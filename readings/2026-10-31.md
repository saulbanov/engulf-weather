## Where this comes from

- Forecasts: the European (ECMWF) and US (NOAA) weather services, plus the National Weather Service once burn night is within a week. Each service runs its forecast dozens of times with small changes; the chances here are how often those versions agree.
- Longer range: NOAA's Climate Prediction Center outlook for 8 to 14 days ahead, which gives the odds of a warmer or colder, wetter or drier stretch than normal. The last 30 days: McComb's airport against its 1991-2020 normals.
- Normal year: the same week of November over the past 30 years, at the site and at McComb, Mississippi, the nearest airport weather station.
- The advice follows fixed rules of thumb, the same every time. It isn't Engulf's official word: the fire safety team decides what burns.
- Not checked: burn bans, flooding on the site itself, and wind under the trees at camp.
